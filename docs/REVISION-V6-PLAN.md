## Narrative and visual decisions

The opening retains both proverb clauses at equal weight, with less height. Transparent torn-paper edges replace the rectangular mat, including in dark mode. The short opening explains that relationships, agency and participation matter to children, staff and parents. A simple crooked blue house with orange roof becomes the favicon.

The house map stays available. The wider map now teaches what changes at provider scale: distributed know-how, onboarding capacity, absolute replacement demand, shared kitchen quality, procurement, family transitions, coordination, learning transfer and net resources. It must not present a larger absolute count as a larger turnover rate or hypothetical savings as achieved savings.

Flow, feedback, comparison, organisation and timeline diagrams use accessible text, captions and mobile layouts. They add no empirical claims that the underlying article does not support.
